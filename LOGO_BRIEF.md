# Terrier AdTech — Logo Brief

## Company in one line
Adtech consultancy for retail, travel, and media platforms. Hands-on, end-to-end. Founder is a 20-year industry veteran (Google, Amazon, Snap, Microsoft).

## Why "Terrier"?
The breed metaphor does the heavy lifting. Terriers are:
- **Tenacious** — they do not let go.
- **Compact and capable** — small package, real power.
- **Working dogs, not show dogs** — bred for the job, not the ribbon.
- **Independent thinkers** — they figure problems out.

The brand voice is dry, confident, editorial. Not cute. Not corporate. Closer to *The Economist* than *MailChimp*.

## Specifically a *Welsh* terrier
Squared, blocky head with a **prominent beard** and a **rectangular muzzle**. Small folded V-ears tipped forward. Sturdy rectangular body, short legs, **docked tail held vertically upright**. Looks like a smaller, scrappier Airedale.

Reference search terms: "Welsh terrier silhouette", "Welsh terrier side profile", "Airedale silhouette" (close cousin, same proportions).

## What the mark needs to do

| Constraint | Detail |
|---|---|
| Smallest size | **16×16 favicon** must still read as a dog |
| Largest size | Building signage / hero illustration |
| Color | Must work as **single-color** (black on cream, cream on black). Color is a bonus, never a requirement. |
| File | SVG, ~24×24 or 32×32 native viewBox |
| Reproduction | Embroidery, foil stamp, laser etch — so no fine detail, no gradients required |
| Lockup | Pairs with wordmark "**Terrier AdTech**" set in **Newsreader** (serif), 500 weight, with "AdTech" in a muted color |

## Brand palette (existing)
- Paper: `#faf7f1`
- Ink: `#1a1714`
- Terra (accent): `#c0593a` — a warm rust/terracotta
- Cream: `#f4efe6`

## Voice the mark should *avoid*
- Cute / cartoonish (no big eyes, no smile, no bone in mouth)
- Tech-bro geometric (no isometric polygons, no "T" monogram with a beveled gradient)
- Generic dog (Lab, Golden — must read specifically *terrier*)
- Hedgehog, rabbit, pig (silhouettes that lose the legs and beard tend here)
- Heraldic crest / 1880s law-firm engraving

## Voice the mark *should* hint at
- Editorial / typographic: comfortable next to serif type
- Hand-cut, slightly imperfect — woodcut, linocut, scratchboard, hot-metal logo
- A bit of dry humor: alert posture, side-eye, cocked head — not goofy, just *paying attention*

## Directions worth trying (give me 5–8)

1. **Linocut profile** — high-contrast side view, thick chunky strokes, a few negative-space gaps for fur texture. The "obvious" direction done well.
2. **Geometric / modular** — body assembled from a few primitive rectangles + triangles. Reads as design-system-friendly. (My current attempt is in this lane but too blocky.)
3. **Single continuous line** — one weight, drawn-without-lifting-the-pen profile. Modern, magazine-y.
4. **Monogram + mark** — an interlocking "T A" where the T's serif doubles as the dog's tail or beard. Wordmark-adjacent.
5. **Just the head** — bust/portrait, three-quarter, beard prominent. Better at favicon size than full body.
6. **Coat-of-arms / seal** — a circular badge containing a tiny terrier silhouette + "TERRIER ADTECH · EST. 2023" set in caps around the rim. Editorial, slightly tongue-in-cheek.
7. **Negative-space cleverness** — the dog's silhouette hides a "T", or the beard *is* a serif.
8. **Bold wordmark only** — no mark; the type does the work. The mark only appears at favicon size as a stripped-down variant.

## Deliverables I'd want from the new project
